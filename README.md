# [Pogopheus](https://soukie007.itch.io/pogopheus)  
"Orph take this"  
"What is it, Heidi?"  
"A pogostick"  
"Uuuh.."  

You are orpheus, Hackclub's most favourite dinosaur. A rogue AI is overloading the servers at Hack Club HQ and it is your job to get over Vermont Hill and reach the servers before it is too late. But remember, you only have one shot!  

# Features  
Fun and satisfying pogoing gameplay! - Charge your space bar to go super high!  
Hand drawn art featuring everyone's favourite mascot  
A really nice bop that plays whilst you are saving Hack Club from disaster  

# How to play  
| Key | Action |
|----|---------|
| W/A/S/D | Move orpheus around!
| Space Bar | Charge orpheus' jump bar for him to jump even higher than normal |  

# How it was made  
This project was written using godot, and the art was made in aseprite
